What changed from today

  • The start page is the feed: newest at the bottom, scroll up for older.
  • All options (expiry, burn after, privacy, syntax) moved to Settings. Defaults: never expires, no burn, public.
  • Text, links, screenshots and files each become their own entry.
  • Pasted or dropped files show as thumbnails above the input before you send.
  • Actions per entry (copy, open, QR, short link, delete) appear on hover, or on tap on the phone.
